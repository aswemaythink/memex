# search

The search family covers design §3.2 and §3.6 for words: what `memex search` prints and its order. That is BM25F ranking with the field weights, English stems, prefix matches for words of 3 or more characters, one-edit matches for words of 5 or more with their `[~variant]` marker, words as runs of Unicode letters and digits with lengths in characters (UNICODE), ids and paths as whole tokens that also match the longer ones they begin at `-`, `/` or `.` (BOUNDARY), the `+` and `!` operators (REQUIRE, EXCLUDE), the groups in their fixed order with their limits, `--in`, the lines of log hits, and the `--json` document. Its prefix is `SRCH`. It also covers the filters (`field:value`, the reserved names and `since:`), the literal terms that are not filters, the roster that `--in` with no words prints, and the `<-N` count of direct citations that marks orphans (design §3.2, §3.6 and §2.3). A group heading names its markers, the `<-N` and `[~variant]` legends (LEGEND), and a zero-hit hint names one filter differently from several (NOHITS). Each case folder holds a small intent layer as its fixture: READMEs, headers, a CASES.md, and where a case needs them a manifest or log entries. SRCH-AGE-01 pins the age of a log line, SRCH-ROSTER-05 the id order of a cases roster, and SRCH-GROUP-03 the name order of manifest groups.
