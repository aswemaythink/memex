# search

The search family covers design §3.2 and §3.6 for words: what `memex search` prints and in what order. That is BM25F ranking with the field weights of PLAN §4, English stems, prefix matches for words of 3 or more characters, one-edit matches for words of 5 or more with their `[~variant]` marker, ids and paths as whole tokens, the groups in their fixed order with their limits, `--in`, the lines of log hits, and the `--json` document. Its prefix is `SRCH`. Filters, the roster and `since:` belong to S3.4, so they are listed as `(planned)` entries and have no folders yet. Each case folder holds a small intent layer as its fixture: READMEs, headers, a CASES.md, and where a case needs them a manifest or log entries.
