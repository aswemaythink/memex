# search

The parts of `memex search` (design §3.2) are split from `../search.rs`, which runs the verb. It groups the docs, ranks each group by the words or lists it by the filters, and limits it. `query.rs` reads the terms: each term's leading `+` or `!` is its operator and comes off first, then the rest is a filter or a literal term whose words are read as usual. The bare words and the `+` words rank the docs, the `+` words must be held, and the `!` words must not be held, so a `!`-only query lists what remains (design §3.2 Operators). `listing.rs` builds a listing or roster group: its docs with logs newest first and then by label, cut to the limit, each roster line with its cited-by count, and each log with its age. `age_of` lives there too, because search.rs shows a log's age on its word hits as well.
