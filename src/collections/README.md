# collections

The collections of design §4.2. A manifest describes one folder of docs that memex does not write itself, and its kind says how the files are read. `frontmatter.rs` is the flat-YAML reader shared by logs and manifests. `manifest.rs` loads and validates the manifests in `.memex/collections/` and dispatches to the reader of each kind, `markdown.rs`, `folder.rs` or `json.rs`, over the roots that `roots.rs` keeps inside the repo root; `roots.rs` also parses the `roots` key. `title.rs` gives every kind its one-liner by one rule. `globs.rs` walks a manifest's roots and matches their files against its globs, for every kind. `json.rs` also holds the scalar reader and the field and doc helpers that `folder.rs` and `markdown.rs` share with it. `testing.rs` holds the test-only helpers that the readers' tests share: a temporary tree, a manifest of a kind and doc labels.
