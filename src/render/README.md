# render

The output of `memex show` and `memex search` (design §3.2, §3.3 and §3.6). `../render.rs` holds the section model that a verb builds: a label, a body, the fields, the children, the citations, the cited-by list and the chain, each with its limit already applied. `text.rs` prints the sections and misses as text, with the blocks in their fixed order, the limits lines and the 120-character cut of one-liners. `json.rs` builds the `{docs, errors}` document of the design's JSON shape. `search.rs` prints `search`'s groups: each group's `(showing N of M)` heading and hit lines, with a log's age, agent and branch, the one-liner cut by `text.rs`'s `clip`, the `[~variant]` markers, and a roster line's `<-N` at its end; and the `--json` document of design §3.6, with scores rounded to 3 decimals (null in a listing or a roster), each filter's values as a list under its name, and a roster hit's `cited_by`.
