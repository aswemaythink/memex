# render

The output of `memex show` and `memex search` (design §3.2, §3.3 and §3.6). `../render.rs` holds the section model that a verb builds: a label, a body, the fields, the children, the citations, the cited-by list and the chain, each with its limit already applied. `text.rs` prints the sections and misses as text, with the blocks in their fixed order, the limits lines, the 120-character cut of one-liners, and the gap legend after `(no README)` and `(no header)` (design §3.3 Gaps). A Markdown child's line shows its title and never a placeholder, so it adds no legend line (design §3.3 Folder). `json.rs` builds the `{docs, errors}` document of the design's JSON shape. `search.rs` prints `search`'s groups: each group's `(showing N of M)` heading (naming the `<-N` and `[~w]` markers its lines use, design §3.2 Markers) and hit lines, with a log's age, agent and branch, the one-liner cut by `text.rs`'s `clip`, and the `[~variant]` markers; and the `--json` document of design §3.6, with scores rounded to 3 decimals. `info.rs` holds the atlas that `info` prints (design §3.5 and §3.6), and `info/` holds its two renderings: `info/text.rs` prints the header, the graph, collections, streams, `to`, `here` and `use` lines, with each label padded to the longest one printed, and a text over 3,000 bytes cuts its longest list line to its first items and a `+N more` ending, until it fits; `info/json.rs` builds the `--json` document of design §3.6 from the same atlas. `check.rs` prints `check`'s report (design §3.5d and §3.6): the scope line with its file, folder and finding counts, each kind's heading with its shown and total counts and its fix line, the `nothing to fix` line when no kind has a finding, and the `--json` document with its kinds and errors.
