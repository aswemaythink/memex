memex show

Read one thing’s intent, then walk its trail: what it cites and what cites it.

What it does

Takes one or more paths or refs. A file prints its header, what it cites (each with its target’s one-liner), who cites it, and the READMEs above it, nearest first. A folder prints its README, then lists its children with their one-liners.

Nobody maintains a link table: citations come from the ids, paths and refs people already write, and are read in both directions. Every label it prints is valid input to show.

Flags

--up N
How many READMEs of the chain to print; --up 0 prints the file alone.
--comments
Also prints the file’s other comment blocks, after the header.
--limit N
Caps the children and cited-by lists.
--json
One JSON document per call, errors included.
